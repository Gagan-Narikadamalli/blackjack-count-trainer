Strategy & Count Guide

Hit
Take another card.
Stand
Take no more cards.
Double
Double the wager and take one final card when allowed.
Split
Divide a matching-value pair into two hands.
Running count
+1 for 2–6, 0 for 7–9, −1 for 10–Ace.
True count
Running count divided by estimated decks remaining.
Important
The count describes shoe composition; it does not reveal the next card.
Rules
Casino rule differences can change basic strategy.
